Supported vocabulary & numeric grammar

HP: at least N hp · over N hp · at most N hp · under N hp · between N and M hp
Years: after YYYY · before YYYY · from YYYY · between YYYY and YYYY
Use digits. Multiple constraints are intersected; contradictions return an error. Other numeric syntax, negation, unknown names, and arbitrary paraphrases are outside the supported scope. Type choices use OR; active fields use AND. Empty filters match all cards.
